Customer Success Manager — Enterprise
FirstAB · Auckland · Hybrid · Permanent

FirstAB provides medical and travel assistance services to insurers, airlines and corporates across Australasia. Our Enterprise Customer Success team owns the relationship with 25 accounts worth NZ$18M in annual recurring revenue.

The role
- Own a portfolio of 8–10 enterprise accounts (insurers and airlines): onboarding, quarterly business reviews, renewal and expansion.
- Carry a net revenue retention target and an expansion quota; forecast renewals monthly.
- Run executive relationships up to COO/CFO level at the client; coordinate delivery, operations and product internally.
- Turn service data (case volumes, SLA performance, NPS) into the account story.

Requirements
- 5+ years in customer success, account management or client services in B2B, with at least 3 years owning enterprise accounts and a renewal or revenue target.
- Evidence of retention and expansion outcomes (numbers).
- Comfortable negotiating contracts and pricing with procurement.
- Insurance, assistance, healthcare or airline industry experience preferred.
- Excellent executive communication; QBR and board-paper quality.

Salary NZ$120,000–140,000 base plus commission (OTE NZ$160,000–180,000). Hybrid from Auckland. Right to work in New Zealand required.
