Senior Business Analyst — Payments Modernisation
ANZ New Zealand · Auckland or Wellington · Hybrid · Permanent

ANZ is replacing its domestic payments platform as part of the industry move to real-time payments (NZ RTP) and ISO 20022 messaging. The Payments Modernisation programme is a three-year, multi-vendor programme with squads across Auckland and Wellington.

The role
As a Senior Business Analyst you will own the requirements for the payment-initiation and confirmation journeys across online banking, the mobile app and the API channel. You'll run workshops with product, operations, fraud and compliance, write user stories and acceptance criteria, model the message flows, and support testing and change teams through to go-live.

Requirements
- 6+ years' business analysis on banking or payments platforms.
- Hands-on with ISO 20022, SWIFT or domestic clearing schemes; you can read a pacs.008.
- Experience on a large multi-vendor programme with formal governance.
- Strong facilitation, process modelling (BPMN) and requirements traceability.
- Working knowledge of AML/CFT and fraud controls as they apply to payments.
- Preferred: experience with real-time payment schemes (NPP, FPS, RTP).

Salary NZ$140,000–165,000 plus bonus and banking benefits. Hybrid working. Applicants must have the right to work in New Zealand.
