Marketing Manager — Digital Growth (LES MILLS+)
Les Mills International · Auckland · Hybrid · Permanent

LES MILLS+ is our direct-to-consumer streaming fitness subscription with members in 100+ countries. We're hiring a Marketing Manager to own subscriber growth for the NZ, AU and UK markets.

The role
- Own the paid and organic acquisition plan across Meta, Google, YouTube and affiliates, with a monthly media budget of ~NZ$400k.
- Run the subscription funnel from trial to paid and from paid to retained: landing pages, trial offers, onboarding emails, win-back.
- Set and report on CAC, trial-to-paid conversion, LTV and churn; run experiments with the growth analyst.
- Brief and manage the creative team and two performance-marketing agencies.

You'll bring
- 5+ years in digital or growth marketing for a consumer subscription or e-commerce brand.
- Hands-on experience managing performance media at scale (NZ$200k+/month) and the agencies that run it.
- Strong funnel analytics; comfortable in GA4, Amplitude and a marketing-automation platform (Braze or similar).
- Proven CAC and conversion improvements you can talk through with numbers.
- A love of fitness helps; a love of growth loops is required.

Salary NZ$120,000–140,000 plus bonus. Hybrid from our Auckland HQ. Applicants must have the right to work in New Zealand.
