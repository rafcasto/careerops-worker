Senior Data Analyst — Immigration Insights
Ministry of Business, Innovation and Employment · Wellington · Hybrid · Permanent · NZ$105,000–125,000

Immigration New Zealand's Insights team turns visa-processing and border data into evidence for ministers, operational leaders and the public.

About the role
You will design and build the analyses and dashboards that track visa processing performance, forecast demand across visa categories, and answer ministerial and OIA requests with defensible numbers. You'll work with data engineers on our Snowflake platform, with policy analysts on definitions, and present findings to senior leaders.

What you'll need
- 4+ years in data analysis with strong SQL and a modern BI tool (Power BI preferred).
- Statistical grounding: forecasting, sampling and the honesty to say what the data can't support.
- R or Python for reproducible analysis.
- Experience explaining findings to non-technical decision-makers under time pressure.
- Public-sector experience, or experience with official statistics, is an advantage.

Applicants must be New Zealand citizens or permanent residents and will undergo a security check.
