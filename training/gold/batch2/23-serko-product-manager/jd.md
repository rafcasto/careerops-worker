Product Manager — Expense (Zeno)
Serko · Auckland · Hybrid · Permanent

You'll bring

- Experience shipping accounting or ERP integrations and working with partner APIs.
- Discovery and PRD craft; comfortable with SQL and product analytics.
- Experience with enterprise customers across multiple markets is a plus; travel domain is a plus.

Salary NZ$140,000–160,000 plus Serko equity. Hybrid from our Auckland office. Right to work in New Zealand required.
