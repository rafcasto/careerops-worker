Delivery Manager — Digital Channels
Spark New Zealand · Auckland · Hybrid · Permanent

Spark's Digital Channels tribe owns the Spark app and website used by 2.5 million customers to buy, pay and get help. We're hiring a Delivery Manager for the Account & Billing squads.

What you'll do
- Run delivery for two squads (16 people across engineering, QA and design) working in two-week sprints on a quarterly planning cadence.
- Own the delivery plan, risks and dependencies across the tribe and with our two offshore engineering partners.
- Manage the squads' delivery budget (NZ$3–4M per year) and vendor statements of work.
- Facilitate planning, retros and quarterly big-room planning; coach squads on flow and predictability.
- Report delivery health to the tribe lead and the PMO.

What you'll bring
- 5+ years in delivery, scrum master or project management roles in software teams; 2+ years running multiple squads.
- Experience managing offshore or vendor delivery partners.
- Budget and SOW management experience.
- Strong facilitation and a data-driven approach to flow (lead time, throughput, predictability).
- Telco, utilities or subscription-billing experience is a plus.

Salary NZ$135,000–155,000 plus benefits. Hybrid from our Auckland office. Right to work in New Zealand required.
