Product Owner — Building Consents Digital
Auckland Council · Auckland CBD · Hybrid · Permanent · NZ$125,000–145,000

Auckland Council processes 20,000 building consent applications a year. The Building Consents Digital squad owns the online application, processing and inspection-booking tools used by applicants, processing officers and 180 inspectors.

The role
- Own the backlog and roadmap for the consents digital products; run discovery with applicants, processing teams and inspectors.
- Turn Building Act and Council process changes into prioritised, testable stories; own acceptance.
- Work with a squad of eight (engineers, QA, designer, BA) and with our SaaS platform vendor on configuration and releases.
- Track processing-time and applicant-satisfaction metrics; report to the consents leadership team.

What you'll bring
- 4+ years as a Product Owner on operational or public-facing digital services.
- Local-government or regulatory domain knowledge — consents, licensing or permitting.
- Experience working with a configurable SaaS platform and its vendor.
- Strong discovery, story-writing and acceptance practice; comfortable with data.
- New Zealand citizenship or residency, or a valid work visa.
