Senior Project Manager — Digital Services
NZ Transport Agency Waka Kotahi · Wellington · Hybrid · Fixed term (18 months) · NZ$135,000–155,000

The Digital Services group delivers the online services New Zealanders use to license vehicles, renew registrations and pay tolls. We're hiring a Senior Project Manager to lead the replacement of the online driver-licensing service.

What you'll do
- Lead an 18-month project (~NZ$9M) delivering a new online licensing service with a SaaS vendor and an internal integration team.
- Own the project plan, budget, risk and dependency management, and reporting to the steering committee and Treasury's Better Business Case gateways.
- Run vendor governance: milestones, acceptance, change control and commercial variations.
- Work with product, security, privacy and the Government Chief Digital Officer's assurance function.

Requirements
- 7+ years project management with at least 3 years delivering software or digital services.
- Experience managing a SaaS or systems-integration vendor under contract.
- Public-sector project governance: business cases, gateway reviews, steering committees.
- PRINCE2 or PMP; familiarity with agile delivery within a governed project.
- New Zealand citizenship or permanent residency is required for security clearance.
