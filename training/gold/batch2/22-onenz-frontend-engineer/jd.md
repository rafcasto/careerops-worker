Senior Frontend Engineer — My One NZ App
One New Zealand · Auckland · Hybrid · Permanent

The My One NZ app is used by 1.4 million customers to manage plans, pay bills and get support. We're hiring a Senior Frontend Engineer for the app squad.

What you'll do
- Build and ship features in our React Native app (TypeScript) for iOS and Android, with a design-system team and a backend-for-frontend layer.
- Own performance and quality: startup time, crash-free rate, accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA) and automated UI testing (Detox).
- Lead frontend design discussions, review code and mentor two mid-level engineers.
- Work with product and design on discovery and with the release train on fortnightly store releases.

Requirements
- 6+ years frontend engineering with 3+ years in React Native (or native iOS/Android plus React) shipping to app stores.
- Deep TypeScript and React fundamentals; state management and offline-tolerant patterns.
- Demonstrated accessibility and performance work on a consumer app.
- Experience with mobile CI/CD, store release management and crash monitoring.
- Telco or subscription-service experience is a plus.

Salary NZ$145,000–170,000. Hybrid from our Auckland office. Right to work in New Zealand required.
