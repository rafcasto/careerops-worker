Product Owner — Client Digital Services
Ministry of Social Development · Wellington · Permanent · Salary band NZ$118,000–139,000

MSD's Client Digital Services group builds and runs MyMSD, the online channel used by over a million New Zealanders to manage benefits, payments and appointments.

About the role
You will be the Product Owner for the MyMSD Payments & Declarations product, working in an agile delivery team of ten (engineers, testers, a designer and a business analyst). You'll own and prioritise the product backlog, write and accept user stories, run sprint ceremonies with the delivery lead, and work with policy and operations teams to turn legislative and operational change into delivered features.

You will need
- At least three years' experience as a Product Owner or equivalent in an agile delivery team.
- Demonstrated experience owning a backlog, writing user stories and acceptance criteria, and prioritising against competing stakeholder needs.
- Experience with digital services for the public, ideally in government or a regulated environment.
- Strong facilitation and stakeholder management skills; comfortable working with policy specialists and frontline staff.
- Understanding of accessibility (WCAG 2.1) and privacy obligations.

Applicants must be New Zealand citizens or permanent residents and will be required to undergo a security clearance.
