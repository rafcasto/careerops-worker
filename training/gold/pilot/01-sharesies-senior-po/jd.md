Senior Product Owner — Investing Experience
Sharesies · Wellington (hybrid, 3 days in office) · Permanent, full-time

Sharesies is a wealth-development platform used by more than 700,000 people across New Zealand and Australia. We're hiring a Senior Product Owner for the Investing Experience squad — the team that owns how people discover, research and buy investments in the app.

What you'll do
- Own the backlog and roadmap for the Investing Experience squad (8 engineers, a designer and a data analyst).
- Run continuous discovery: interviews, usability testing and experiment design with our research team.
- Define and track OKRs; report weekly on funnel metrics from first deposit to first investment.
- Work with Compliance on features that touch the Financial Markets Conduct Act and our disclosure obligations.
- Partner with engineering leads on delivery, release planning and incident reviews.

What we're looking for
- 5+ years in product ownership or product management in a consumer app, ideally fintech or investing.
- Proven record of running discovery and shipping features that moved a measured metric.
- Comfortable with SQL and product analytics (we use Amplitude and BigQuery).
- Experience working with regulated products or alongside a compliance function.
- Strong written communication; you'll write the PRDs and the customer-facing release notes.

Nice to have: experience with KiwiSaver or managed funds; a background in behavioural design.

Salary band NZ$150,000–175,000 plus employee share scheme. Hybrid: Wellington office three days per week. Applicants must hold the right to work in New Zealand.
