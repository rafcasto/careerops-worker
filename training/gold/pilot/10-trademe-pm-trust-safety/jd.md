Product Manager — Marketplace Trust & Safety
Trade Me · Wellington or Auckland · Hybrid · Permanent

Trade Me is where New Zealand buys, sells and finds. The Trust & Safety squad builds the systems that keep 5 million members safe: listing moderation, seller verification, fraud detection and member reporting.

The role
You'll own the product roadmap for member-facing trust tools (reporting, seller badges, dispute flows) and the internal moderation console used by our 40-person Trust & Safety operations team. You'll work with a squad of seven engineers, a designer and two data scientists who maintain our fraud-scoring models.

You'll need
- 4+ years product management on a marketplace, classifieds or two-sided platform.
- Experience partnering with an operations or support team and building internal tools they use every day.
- Comfort with data: you can define a fraud or abuse metric, query it, and explain a model's outputs to a non-technical audience.
- Sound judgement on policy trade-offs between member safety, seller experience and revenue.
- Nice to have: experience with ML-driven moderation, identity verification vendors, or consumer-protection regulation.

Salary NZ$135,000–160,000 plus bonus. Hybrid from Wellington or Auckland. Right to work in New Zealand required.
