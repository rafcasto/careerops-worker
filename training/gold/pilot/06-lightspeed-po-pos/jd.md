Product Owner — Point of Sale (NZ)
Lightspeed · Auckland · Hybrid · Permanent

Lightspeed's retail POS is used by thousands of stores across New Zealand and Australia. Our Auckland team owns the in-store selling experience: the register, inventory lookups, returns and hardware integrations (printers, scanners, payment terminals).

Responsibilities
- Own and prioritise the backlog for the Register squad (six engineers, one QA, one designer).
- Write user stories and acceptance criteria; run refinement, sprint planning and reviews.
- Gather requirements from retailers, support and sales; validate solutions in stores before release.
- Track adoption and defect metrics; work with support on the top issues each month.

Requirements
- 2+ years as a Product Owner, Business Analyst or similar in an agile software team.
- Retail or point-of-sale domain knowledge — you understand how a store runs.
- Clear written communication; experience with Jira or similar.
- Ability to work with hardware partners and payment providers on integration issues.

Salary NZ$105,000–125,000. Hybrid working from our Auckland office. Applicants must be legally entitled to work in New Zealand.
