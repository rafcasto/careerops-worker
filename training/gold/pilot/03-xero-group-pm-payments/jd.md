Group Product Manager — Payments
Xero · Auckland or Wellington · Hybrid · Permanent

Xero helps 4 million small businesses get paid. The Payments group owns invoice payments, bank feeds reconciliation and our partnerships with Stripe, GoCardless and local acquirers.

About the role
As Group Product Manager you will lead three product managers and set the direction for the Payments group across NZ, Australia and the UK. You'll own the group's OKRs and its P&L contribution, run quarterly planning with engineering and design leadership, and represent Payments to the executive team and to partners.

Requirements
- 8+ years in product management, including 2+ years leading and developing other product managers.
- Track record of owning a product P&L or revenue target in a SaaS or payments business.
- Deep understanding of payments — acquiring, payment methods, reconciliation, pricing and regulation across multiple markets.
- Experience negotiating and managing commercial partnerships.
- Executive-level communication; comfortable presenting strategy to a C-suite.

Salary: NZ$220,000–260,000 base, plus bonus and equity. Hybrid working from our Auckland or Wellington offices. Right to work in New Zealand required.
