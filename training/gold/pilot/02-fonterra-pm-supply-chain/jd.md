Product Manager — Digital Supply Chain
Fonterra · Auckland (Penrose) or Hamilton · Hybrid · Permanent

Fonterra's Digital Supply Chain team builds the internal platforms that move 22 billion litres of milk a year from farm to customer: milk collection scheduling, tanker routing, plant intake and outbound logistics.

The role
You will be the product manager for the Collection & Routing product — the scheduling and routing tools used by our transport planners and 400 tanker drivers. You'll own the roadmap, run discovery with planners and drivers across the country, and work with a team of six engineers and a data scientist to deliver improvements that reduce kilometres driven and missed collections.

You'll bring
- 3+ years of product management, product ownership or senior business analysis on operational or logistics software.
- Experience turning field research into prioritised backlogs; comfortable spending time at plants and in trucks.
- Working knowledge of optimisation or scheduling problems (routing, capacity planning) and the data behind them.
- Ability to write clear requirements and acceptance criteria; SQL for your own analysis.
- Stakeholder management across operations, transport and IT.

Preferred: dairy, FMCG or transport industry experience; familiarity with SAP TM or similar.

We offer flexible hybrid working, a strong learning budget and Fonterra's benefits package. Salary discussed at interview. Candidates must be eligible to work in New Zealand.
