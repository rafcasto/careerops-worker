Product Manager — Rancher App
Halter · Auckland (Parnell) · Hybrid, with regular farm visits · Permanent

Halter makes smart collars and an app that let farmers shift and manage cattle remotely. We're growing fast in New Zealand, Australia and the US.

The role
You'll own the Halter mobile app for farmers: the daily tool used to move mobs, set virtual fences, monitor animal health alerts and plan grazing. You'll work with a squad of eight engineers, two designers and a data scientist, and spend at least two days a month on farms with our customers and field team.

What you'll need
- 4+ years product management on a mobile app with a large active user base.
- Hands-on discovery skills: you've run interviews and usability sessions and changed direction because of them.
- Strong quantitative instincts — you define the metrics, you read the dashboards.
- Experience shipping features that depend on hardware, firmware or connectivity constraints is a plus.
- Willingness to travel to farms and to work early when farmers do.

Bonus: agriculture background, experience with offline-first apps, or a track record in a scale-up.

Compensation NZ$140,000–165,000 plus equity. Must have the right to work in New Zealand.
