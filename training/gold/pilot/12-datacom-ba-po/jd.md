Business Analyst / Product Owner — Local Government Platforms
Datacom · Wellington · Hybrid · Permanent

Datacom's Local Government practice builds and runs Datascape and related platforms for more than 30 councils across New Zealand and Australia: rates, consents, licensing and customer request management.

About the role
You'll work as a business analyst and product owner across two or three council customers at a time: gathering and documenting requirements, shaping backlog items with our delivery teams, running workshops with council staff, and owning the product backlog for the customer-request-management module.

What you'll bring
- 3+ years as a business analyst on enterprise software, with some experience owning or shaping a product backlog.
- Local-government or public-sector domain knowledge — consents, rates, licensing or customer service processes.
- Strong workshop facilitation and documentation skills (user stories, process maps, acceptance criteria).
- Comfortable travelling to customer sites across NZ around one week per month.
- Familiarity with agile delivery and tools such as Azure DevOps or Jira.

Salary NZ$110,000–130,000. Hybrid working from our Wellington office. Applicants must have the right to work in New Zealand.
