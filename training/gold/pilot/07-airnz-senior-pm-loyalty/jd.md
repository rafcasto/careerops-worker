Senior Product Manager — Airpoints
Air New Zealand · Auckland (The Hub, Fanshawe St) · Hybrid · Permanent

Airpoints is New Zealand's largest loyalty programme with more than 4 million members. The Loyalty Digital team owns the Airpoints experience across the Air NZ app and website: earning, redeeming, status tracking and our partner earn network.

What you'll own
- Product strategy and roadmap for the Airpoints member experience (earn, burn, status).
- A squad of nine engineers, a designer and an analyst, plus a close partnership with the Loyalty commercial team on partner offers and campaigns.
- Member-experience OKRs: redemption rate, engagement with partner earn, and app NPS for loyalty journeys.
- Discovery with members and with partners (banks, retailers, fuel).

You'll bring
- 6+ years product management on a consumer digital product, with at least 2 years in loyalty, rewards or subscription products.
- Experience partnering with a commercial team on offers, pricing or partner economics.
- A record of moving a customer metric (engagement, retention, redemption) with evidence.
- Comfort with experimentation, product analytics and SQL.
- Excellent stakeholder skills across commercial, marketing and technology teams.

Salary NZ$160,000–185,000 plus staff travel benefits. Hybrid working from our Auckland office. You must have the right to live and work in New Zealand.
