Product Owner — Mission Operations Software
Rocket Lab · Auckland (Mt Wellington) · On-site · Permanent

Rocket Lab's Mission Operations Software team builds the tools our flight controllers use to plan, rehearse and execute Electron and Neutron launches: telemetry displays, procedure execution, anomaly logging and post-flight analysis.

The role
You'll be the product owner for the procedure-execution and anomaly-logging tools, working on site with flight operations engineers and a software team of seven. You'll prioritise the backlog against launch-campaign timelines, write user stories with the operators who use the tools during countdown, and sit console-side during rehearsals and launches.

Requirements
- 4+ years as a product owner or technical product manager on mission-critical, real-time or safety-related software (aerospace, defence, energy, medical devices, industrial control).
- Ability to read telemetry and procedures; comfortable with engineering documentation and hazard analysis.
- Experience working under launch-campaign or equivalent hard-deadline conditions.
- On-site availability in Auckland including some launch-window shifts (nights and weekends).
- Due to export-control regulations (ITAR/EAR), applicants must be New Zealand or Australian citizens, or permanent residents; other applicants require case-by-case approval which may not be possible.

Salary NZ$130,000–155,000. Relocation assistance is not available for this role.
